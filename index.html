How to Play

Objective

Tigers try to capture 5 Goats. Goats try to trap all three Tigers so they cannot move.

Pieces

3 Tigers   15 Goats

Board

23 connected positions. Pieces move only along the drawn lines.

First Move

The Goat player always moves first.

Goat Placement

For the first 15 Goat turns, the Goat player places one new Goat on any empty position instead of moving.

Tiger Movement

A Tiger moves one step to an adjacent, empty, connected position.

Tiger Capture

A Tiger jumps in a straight line over an adjacent Goat and lands on the empty position beyond it. The Goat is removed.

Goat Movement

Once all 15 Goats are placed, the Goat player moves one Goat to an adjacent empty position each turn.

Winning

5 Goats captured → Tigers win. No legal Tiger moves → Goats win.

Draw

After 30 moves each with no winner, either player may claim a draw.

About

Aadu Puli Aattam (ஆடுபுலி ஆட்டம்) is a traditional two-player strategy board game from Tamil Nadu, India, played for generations on courtyards, temple floors, and palm-leaf boards.

Three Tigers hunt fifteen Goats across 23 connected positions, while the Goats try to work together to trap every Tiger. This digital edition recreates the traditional rules with three levels of computer opponents, from a gentle teacher to a calculating minimax-powered strategist.

This is an original implementation built for the web — no external assets were copied from any reference site.
